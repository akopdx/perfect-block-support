Perfect Block - Support

About

Perfect Block is a block puzzle game where you place blocks on a board, clear lines, and aim for Perfect Clears!

How to Play

  • Drag and drop blocks onto the board
  • Complete a row or column to clear it
  • Clear the entire board for a Perfect Clear and Fever Mode
  • Chain combos to trigger Mini Fever
