Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

Overview

Perfect Block ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App:

  • Does not require an account or login
  • Does not collect personal information
  • Does not use analytics or tracking
  • Does not use cookies
  • Does not contain advertisements
  • Does not require an internet connection

Local Data

The App stores game progress (high scores and challenge level completion) locally on your device using standard system storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
